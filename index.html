Privacy Notice

Who we are. Safetech Resourcing Ltd ("we", "us") is the data controller for personal data you share with us. Contact: syed.tahir@safetechresourcing.co.uk.

What we collect. Information you send us, such as your name, contact details, CV, employment history, qualifications, right-to-work status and availability, and details of client enquiries.

Why we use it. To assess your suitability for roles, introduce you to clients (only with your agreement), and manage our recruitment services. Our lawful bases are legitimate interests, taking steps towards a contract, and compliance with legal obligations.

Who we share it with. Prospective clients for specific roles you have agreed to be put forward for, and trusted service providers who help us operate (for example, email hosting). We never sell your data.

How long we keep it. Only as long as necessary for recruitment purposes. If we have had no contact with you for 24 months, we will delete or anonymise your data.

Special category data. Please do not include sensitive information (for example health, religion or ethnicity) in your CV unless it is relevant to a reasonable adjustment you want us to know about.

Transfers. Your data is stored with UK-based or adequately protected service providers. If data is transferred outside the UK, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place as required by UK data protection law.

Your rights. Under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your data, object to its use, or request a copy in a portable format, by emailing us. We will respond within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies. This website does not set cookies or use analytics or tracking, so no cookie banner is required under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). The page loads its styling and font from third-party content delivery networks (cdn.tailwindcss.com and Google Fonts), which will see your IP address as part of normal web traffic, and is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security.
